# Limitations and open questions

> The historical name "Mastery of Time" reflects the project's informal
> name; the formal scientific designation is the
> **Temporon-Mediated Theory (TMT)**.

The manuscript submitted to *Physical Review D* is deliberately restricted
in scope so that the central derivation and the SPARC validation stand on
their own merit. Three limitations are explicitly acknowledged.

## Merging clusters (Bullet Cluster)

The Bullet Cluster and other merging clusters provide a stringent test for
any alternative to dark matter, because the gravitational-lensing peaks
coincide with the stellar component rather than with the X-ray gas.
Compatibility with these data, within TMT, requires a **density-dependent
matter coupling** \( \kappa(\rho) \) that activates the temporon
selectively in dense rather than diffuse baryonic regions. A complete
numerical analysis of the two-dimensional lensing map is the subject of a
forthcoming paper.

## Cosmology and the cosmic microwave background

The cosmological consequences of the action — density-dependent late-time
expansion, the behaviour of the temporon at the recombination epoch — are
not treated quantitatively in the galactic manuscript. The
CMB-compatibility argument is currently perturbative; a full Boltzmann
implementation (CAMB or CLASS, with the coupled
\( \delta\psi\!-\!\delta\rho_{m} \) system) is in progress.

## Lagrangian parameters

The fundamental Lagrangian parameters — \( \lambda \), \( v \), \( \xi \),
\( \kappa \), \( \Lambda_{0} \) — are at this stage **calibrated against
observations** rather than derived from a deeper microphysical theory.
Reducing their number, or anchoring them to a fundamental physical scale,
remains an open question.

---

These three limitations are stated explicitly in the manuscript itself.
Acknowledging them openly is part of the approach: a theory is judged as
much by the clarity of what it has not yet established as by its results.
