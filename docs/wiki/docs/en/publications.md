# Publications

> The historical name "Mastery of Time" reflects the project's informal
> name; the formal scientific designation is the
> **Temporon-Mediated Theory (TMT)**.

## Peer-reviewed (in review)

**Després Asselin, P.-O. (2026).** *Galactic rotation curves from a
scalar-tensor theory with double-well potential: derivation of the
transition-radius scaling \( r_{c}\propto M_{\rm bary}^{1/2} \).*
Manuscript submitted to *Physical Review D* (May 2026).

This manuscript derives, from the action, the effective mass profile of
disk galaxies and the scaling law \( r_{c}\propto M_{\rm bary}^{1/2} \),
then confronts it with the SPARC sample of 168 galaxies.

## Companion papers in preparation

The cosmological consequences of the same action — density-dependent
late-time expansion, the acoustic-peak structure of the cosmic microwave
background, the treatment of merging clusters (Bullet Cluster) — are the
subject of a companion paper currently in preparation. It will develop the
full FLRW formulation and the modified-Boltzmann implementation
(CAMB / CLASS).
