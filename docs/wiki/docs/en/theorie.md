# Theory

> The historical name "Mastery of Time" reflects the project's informal
> name; the formal scientific designation is the
> **Temporon-Mediated Theory (TMT)**.

## The action

TMT rests on a covariant four-term action,

$$ S = S_{\rm EH} + S_{\psi} + S_{\xi} + S_{m}, $$

comprising the Einstein–Hilbert term with bare cosmological constant
\( \Lambda_{0} \), the kinetic and potential terms of the scalar field
\( \psi \), the non-minimal curvature coupling
\( S_{\xi} = -\tfrac{1}{2}\int d^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\;\xi\psi^{2}R \), and the
matter action. This action is a special case of the Bergmann–Wagoner family
of scalar-tensor theories and overlaps the Horndeski subclass. Its
distinguishing feature is the choice of a **double-well potential**

$$ V(\psi) = \frac{\lambda}{4}\,(\psi^{2}-v^{2})^{2}. $$

## The temporon field

The *temporon* \( \psi \) is a single real scalar field — the only new
degree of freedom introduced by the theory. Varying the action with respect
to the metric reveals an **effective gravitational coupling** that depends
locally on the condensate,

$$ G_{\rm eff}(\psi) = \frac{G}{1 - 8\pi G\,\xi\psi^{2}}, $$

while variation with respect to \( \psi \) yields a modified Klein–Gordon
equation with a curvature term \( \xi R\,\psi \) and a coupling to the
matter density.

## Two physical regimes

For static or slowly varying configurations, the temporon equilibrium
admits two solutions:

- **Symmetric phase** \( \psi \approx 0 \) — stable when the
  curvature-mass term dominates; this is the cosmological regime and that
  of low-density environments.
- **Condensed phase** \( \psi \approx \pm v \) — stable when symmetry
  breaking dominates; this is the regime of dense baryonic environments,
  notably the interior of galaxies.

The boundary between the two phases defines a **transition density**
\( \rho_{\rm tr} \). With the SPARC-calibrated parameters,
\( \rho_{\rm tr}(z=0)\approx 0.3\,\rho_{c} \). Because curvature grows
strongly with redshift, the transition density becomes very large at early
epochs, driving the temporon back toward the symmetric phase in the early
universe.

## Post-Newtonian limit

In the post-Newtonian regime, expansion of the metric and of \( \psi \)
around the cosmic background yields the PPN parameters \( \gamma \) and
\( \beta \). Solar-system constraints (Cassini, lunar laser ranging,
MESSENGER) are satisfied for \( \xi v^{2} < 10^{-5}\,M_{\rm Pl}^{2} \)
through a chameleon-type screening mechanism: in the solar system, the high
local density gives the temporon a large effective mass that suppresses any
observable deviation.

## Heuristic motivations

> **Note.** This section collects intuitive images that guided the
> development of the project. They are **not** primitive concepts of the
> theory: the rigorous formulation is entirely contained in the
> scalar-tensor action above. These images are kept here for pedagogical
> and historical value only.

In its early phase, the project described the effect of the field through
heuristic notions — for example a "time dilation" associated with the
gravitational potential, or a mass-like quantity built from
\( \Phi/c^{2} \). These formulations should be understood as **heuristic
interpretations** of rigorous equations, not as independent postulates. The
testable physical content of the theory is what derives directly from the
action and is confronted with data in the
[Galactic results](resultats-galactiques.md) section.
